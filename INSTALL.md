# Putting SloopyTV on your Fire Stick

You do this once. After that the app updates itself.

Works on Fire TV Sticks and Fire TV boxes running Fire OS. It does **not** work on the newest
"Fire TV Stick 4K Select" (the one with Vega OS), because Amazon blocks adding apps on it.

## 1. Let the Fire Stick add apps

1. On the Fire Stick home screen, go to **Settings** (the gear on the right).
2. Open **My Fire TV**, then **About**.
3. Highlight **Fire TV Stick** at the top and press the **select button** (the big round one) **seven times**. A little message says you are now a developer. That's fine.
4. Press **Back** once. You'll now see **Developer options**. Open it.
5. Turn on **Apps from Unknown Sources**. On newer Fire Sticks it's called **Install unknown apps** and shows a list of apps instead; if so, come back here after step 2 and turn **Downloader** on. Leave **ADB debugging** off.

## 2. Get the Downloader app

1. Go back to the home screen and open the **search** (magnifying glass).
2. Search for **Downloader**. Pick the orange one by **AFTVnews**. Press **Get** or **Download**.
3. If step 1 showed **Install unknown apps**, go back there now (Settings › My Fire TV › Developer options) and turn **Downloader** on.

## 3. Install SloopyTV

1. Open **Downloader**. Say OK to anything it asks the first time.
2. In the box at the top, type this code and press **Go**:

   **(code coming soon)**

   If the code doesn't work, type this instead, exactly:
   `github.com/elwoodradley/sloopytv/releases/latest/download/sloopytv.apk`

3. It downloads for a few seconds, then asks **Install**. Press it. Then **Done**.
4. Downloader may offer to delete the file. Say yes.

## 4. Open it

SloopyTV is now in your apps. On the home screen, press and hold the **Home** button on the
remote, choose **Apps**, and you'll find it. Hold select on it to move it to the front row.

The first screen shows a square code. Point your phone's camera at it, tap the link that pops
up, and type or paste your TV login on your phone. Done. If you'd rather, there are boxes on the
TV screen too.

## Later

When a new version is out, a line appears at the bottom of the picture. Go to **Settings ›
About** inside SloopyTV and press **Install**. The very first time, the Fire Stick asks you to
allow SloopyTV to update itself: turn that on, press Back, press Install again.
